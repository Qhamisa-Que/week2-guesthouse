About us

Molo, and welcome. Ekhaya means "home" in isiXhosa, and that is exactly what we want you to feel. We are a family-run guesthouse in Southernwood, five minutes from the Nelson Mandela Museum and ten from Walter Sisulu University. Whether you are visiting a student, in town for business, or on your way to the Wild Coast, you will find a clean room, a warm breakfast, and people who are glad you came.

  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Secure parking
  • Backup power
  • Breakfast included
